Water Treatment SCADA Applications

Water treatment facilities rely on sophisticated SCADA systems to monitor and control the entire treatment process, from raw water intake to final distribution. These systems provide real-time data on water quality parameters, equipment status, and process efficiency, enabling operators to make informed decisions and ensure the delivery of safe drinking water.

Our water treatment SCADA solutions are designed to address the specific challenges faced by water utilities, including regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and resource optimization. By implementing advanced monitoring and control capabilities, we help water treatment facilities improve water quality, reduce operational costs, and enhance overall system reliability.

With over four decades of experience as systems integrators, we have the expertise to design, implement, and maintain SCADA systems that meet the unique requirements of water treatment facilities of all sizes. Our team works closely with facility operators to ensure that each system is tailored to their specific needs and provides the functionality required for efficient operation.

Key Components of Water SCADA Systems

Intake Monitoring

Our SCADA systems provide comprehensive monitoring of raw water intake, including flow rates, water levels, and quality parameters. This enables operators to optimize the intake process and respond quickly to changes in source water conditions.

Chemical Dosing Control

Precise control of chemical dosing is critical for effective water treatment. Our SCADA systems automate the dosing process based on real-time water quality measurements, ensuring optimal chemical usage and consistent treatment results.

Filtration Monitoring

Filtration is a key step in water treatment. Our SCADA systems monitor filter performance, including flow rates, pressure differentials, and turbidity levels, enabling operators to optimize filter operation and schedule maintenance activities.

Distribution Management

Effective distribution management ensures that treated water reaches consumers safely and efficiently. Our SCADA systems monitor distribution networks, including pump stations, storage tanks, and pressure zones, to maintain system integrity and service quality.
