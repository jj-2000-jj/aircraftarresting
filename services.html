Our Systems Integration Expertise

As a systems integrator with over four decades of experience, we specialize in designing, implementing, and maintaining SCADA systems for water and wastewater facilities of all sizes. Our team of experts works closely with facility operators to create customized solutions that meet specific operational requirements and regulatory standards.

We understand the unique challenges faced by water and wastewater treatment facilities and have developed solutions that address these challenges while providing the flexibility to adapt to changing conditions and requirements. Our comprehensive approach includes system design, hardware and software integration, installation, commissioning, training, and ongoing support.

Our partnership with GBL Data allows us to leverage the latest technologies and industry best practices to deliver reliable, scalable, and user-friendly SCADA systems that enhance operational efficiency and reduce costs.

Our Services

System Design & Engineering

Our engineering team works closely with facility operators to design SCADA systems that meet their specific requirements. We consider factors such as process complexity, regulatory requirements, and future expansion plans to create systems that are both effective and scalable.

  • • Requirements analysis and system specification
  • • Hardware and software selection
  • • Network architecture design
  • • Control panel design and fabrication

Implementation & Integration

Our implementation team handles all aspects of SCADA system installation and integration, ensuring seamless operation with existing equipment and processes. We follow industry best practices and rigorous quality control procedures to deliver reliable and effective systems.

  • • Hardware installation and configuration
  • • Software programming and customization
  • • Integration with existing systems
  • • Testing and commissioning

Training & Support

We provide comprehensive training for facility operators and maintenance staff, ensuring they have the knowledge and skills needed to effectively use and maintain the SCADA system. Our ongoing support services ensure that systems continue to operate reliably and efficiently.

  • • Operator and administrator training
  • • System documentation and manuals
  • • 24/7 technical support
  • • Preventive maintenance programs

System Upgrades & Expansion

As facilities grow and technology evolves, we help clients upgrade and expand their SCADA systems to meet changing requirements. Our approach ensures minimal disruption to ongoing operations while providing access to the latest technologies and capabilities.

  • • System assessment and upgrade planning
  • • Legacy system migration
  • • Capacity expansion
  • • Technology updates and enhancements
